Module 3

Understand what the customer is being offered

Differentiate routine maintenance from repairs. Never guess a vehicle's service schedule or final bill.

Free / complimentary service

May include defined inspections and maintenance operations. Confirm due date, kilometres, inclusions and eligibility in the owner's manual or DMS.

Paid periodic service

Scheduled maintenance after complimentary services. Scope and charges vary by model, fuel type, age, kilometres and applicable schedule.

Running repair

A reported concern outside routine periodic maintenance. Diagnosis comes first; explain parts and labour separately.

The service odometer

Reference intervals from supplied training material. Tap a milestone.

Verification required: These are reference notes, not a universal Maruti schedule or current price list. Verify the model/fuel-specific maintenance chart, inclusions, taxes and charges in DMS / owner's manual before advising.

Module 4

The customer journey inside the service centre

The call centre sets expectations; the workshop fulfils them. Tap a station, or let the car drive the route.

Agent handover standard: Send complete customer and vehicle details, stated concern, agreed date/time, pickup request, campaign discussion and any special promise. Never promise a slot or service that the workshop has not confirmed.
Module 5

The 7-step call framework

Prepare, introduce, ask permission, discover, recommend, book, then close and log.

Professional opening script

Replace the bracketed items with verified details.

Good morning/afternoon. May I speak with Mr./Ms. [Customer Name]?
My name is [Agent Name], calling from Rukmani Motors.
I am calling regarding your Maruti Suzuki [Model], registration [Number].
As per the service information available to us, your vehicle may be due for its next scheduled service. May I take a minute to confirm a few details?
We would be happy to help you explore a convenient service appointment at our authorised workshop.
Privacy and consent: Use customer data only for an authorised business purpose. Verify the person before sharing vehicle details. Respect do-not-call requests and company contact policies.

Role-play and knowledge check

Use the scenarios in pairs: one agent, one customer, then switch roles.

Customer bought a Baleno elsewhere and prefers the selling dealer. Respect the relationship and explain Rukmani as an alternative.

Customer asks whether the labour discount covers parts and taxes. Explain only verified terms.

C · “I had a bad experience.”

Customer is reluctant to trust another workshop. Listen, document and offer manager review.
